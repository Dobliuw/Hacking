# Future Perfect

The future perfect tense is only used in a few situations, but it's still good to know it. It is made with the *future simple* of `have` (**Will have**) and the *past participle* (3th column). For regular past participles add *ed* to the verb like in other tenses.

- **Positive**:  Sub + `will` + `have` + `PP`.
	- *Short Way*: Sub`'ll` + `have` + `PP`.
- **Negative**: Sub + `will` + `not have` + `PP` 
	- *Short Way*: Sub + `won't` + `PP`

**Positive**:
By six pm tonight...
- I will have finished this book
- You will have studied the English tenses
- He will have cooked dinner.
- They will have left Japan.

**Positive short**:
- I'll have finised this book.
- You'll have studied the English tenses.
- .....

**Negative**:
By next week....
- I will not have finished this book.
- You will not have studiede the Enlgish tenses.

**Questions**: (*Yes* / *No*)
By next year....
- Will I have finished writing this book?
- Will you have studied all the English verb tenses?
- Will he have graduated?

**Questions**: (*Wh*)
- When will you have finished writing this book?
